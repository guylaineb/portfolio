Project Overview

Chime is a music streaming web application that aims to make music more accessible to everyone. Unlike many platforms locked behind expensive subscriptions, Chime provides free streaming of music, a growing library of royalty-free tracks, and an affordable premium plan for listeners who want extra features without breaking the bank.

Timeline

From explorations to final designs in approximately 4 weeks.

The Challenge

Most music streaming platforms are costly, restrict free features, and lack easy access to royalty-free music. Free users face heavy limitations and ads, while royalty-free music is locked behind complex licensing and high costs. This creates barriers for budget-conscious listeners, students, and content creators, while independent artists struggle to be discovered.

Objectives

Chime's goal is to break down these barriers by offering:

  • Free music streaming with fair access for all users.

  • A royalty-free library that empowers content creators and supports artists.

  • A low-cost premium plan with enhanced features like ad-free listening, offline playback, and high-quality audio.

  • A simple, responsive, and user-first design that makes discovering and enjoying music effortless.

Chime ultimately seeks to create a fair, inclusive, and affordable music ecosystem where listeners and artists thrive together.

Process

This category details the step-by-step approach taken during the project, including research, planning, design, development, testing, and optimization phases.

Research & Define

In the early stages of designing Chime, I focused on understanding the problems users face with existing music streaming platforms. Through research and competitive analysis, I identified recurring pain points. To ground these findings, I created user personas representing budget-conscious listeners, indie music fans, and creators who rely on royalty-free music. Mapping their journeys helped clarify where frustrations occurred and where opportunities existed. From this, I was able to frame the core challenge: How might we design a streaming platform that is affordable, inclusive, and easy to use, while also supporting artists and creators?

Ideate & Prototype

After understanding user problems, I began turning research insights into tangible design ideas. I first created a sitemap to establish clear navigation flows and ensure users could easily discover music, create playlists, and explore subscription options. Next, I sketched paper wireframes to quickly explore different layouts and interaction patterns, focusing on simplicity and accessibility. These sketches were then translated into a low-fidelity prototype, which allowed me to test user flows and gather early feedback before moving into high-fidelity designs. This stage was crucial in shaping Chime's foundation, ensuring that usability and clarity were prioritized from the very beginning.

Test & Deliver

Moving to the test and deliver stage, I translated insights and wireframes into polished UI designs with a clean, modern look that emphasized simplicity and ease of use. I incorporated accessibility considerations to make the app inclusive for all users. To validate the design, I conducted a usability study, gathering feedback on the user flow. Based on these insights, I made several revisions to improve clarity, streamline interactions, and ensure a smoother overall experience. These revisions were brought together in a high-fidelity prototype, showcasing realistic interactions and responsive layouts.

User Research

To better understand the audience for Chime, I conducted a mix of surveys and informal interviews with potential users, including students, indie music fans, and small content creators. The goal was to uncover their listening habits, frustrations with current streaming services, and expectations from a fair, affordable platform.

Pain Points

High Subscription Costs

Premium plans are too expensive for students and budget-conscious listeners.

Lack of Royalty-Free Music

Content creators struggle to find affordable, easy-to-license tracks for their projects.

Limited Indie Artist Exposure

Independent musicians have few opportunities to be discovered alongside mainstream artists.

Restrictive Free Access

Free tiers often include constant ads, limited skips, and reduced audio quality.

Cluttered Interfaces

Overloaded designs make navigation confusing and discovery less enjoyable.

This analysis highlighted a clear opportunity for Chime: to position itself as a budget-friendly, inclusive alternative by offering free streaming, a library of royalty-free music, and a simpler, less cluttered user experience.

Ideate & Prototype

Sitemap

I created a sitemap to establish clear navigation and user flows for Chime. It outlined the main sections: Homepage, Library, For You, Discover, Login/Signup, and Settings, ensuring users could easily browse music, manage playlists, and explore subscription options. This helped simplify the structure and guide the design of intuitive pathways throughout the app.

Paper Wireframes

To quickly explore layout ideas, I began with paper wireframes. Sketching multiple variations of the home screen, discovery page, and playlist flow allowed me to experiment freely without being limited to digital tools.

Mid-fidelity Wireframes

After refining my paper sketches, I translated them into a mid-fidelity digital prototype to visualize Chime's core user flows. This version focused on structure and functionality rather than visuals, allowing me to test navigation across key screens. By keeping the design minimal, I was able to quickly validate usability, gather feedback, and make iterative improvements before moving on to high-fidelity designs.

UI Design

The UI design for Chime centered on a clean, modern aesthetic that puts music at the forefront. I used a black background to create contrast and highlight album artwork, paired with purple accents to convey creativity and energy. To balance the palette, I added turquoise highlights that bring freshness and vibrancy to key actions. Alongside the interface, I also designed the Chime logo, a simple yet distinctive mark inspired by music notes and pixels, reflecting the app's focus on accessibility and music discovery. Together with rounded elements, consistent spacing, and clear typography further enhance usability, resulting in an interface that feels both stylish and intuitive.

Accessibility Considerations

Accessibility was a guiding principle in designing Chime, ensuring that music streaming is enjoyable and inclusive for all users. Accessibility is not just about compliance; it's about making the app usable, welcoming, and fair for everyone. Some considerations I've taken note of:

Color Contrast

Black background with purple/turquoise accents designed for WCAG-compliant contrast

Icon + Text Labels

Clear iconography paired with labels for better comprehension

Touch Targets

Buttons and controls sized for easy use on both desktop and mobile

Consistent Navigation

Logical structure and predictable patterns across all screens

Alt Text Support

Space for album covers and artwork descriptions for screen readers

Usability Study

To evaluate Chime's design, I conducted a usability study with a small group of target users who tested the high-fidelity prototype. Participants were asked to complete core tasks such as searching for a song, downloading, and exploring the premium upgrade flow. The study revealed valuable insights.

Key Insights

Song/Playlist Options

Users wanted more flexibility when managing songs and playlists

Search Function

Users suggested an easier way to find songs without typing

Playback Controls

Users wanted more control over how they listen

Revisions

Based on the insights gathered from the usability study, I made several key revisions to enhance Chime's overall experience. These revisions ensured that Chime not only looked visually polished but also aligned more closely with user needs and expectations.

Revision #1: Expanded Song Options

To give users more flexibility and control, I added an expanded set of options for each song, including Add to Queue, Go To Artist, Go To Album, Add to Playlist, and Copy Song Link. These additions make it easier for listeners to manage their music seamlessly, discover related content, and share tracks with others, ultimately creating a richer and more engaging experience within Chime.

Revision #2: Voice Search

To make discovering music faster and more accessible, I integrated a voice search feature in Chime. This allows users to simply speak the name of a song, artist, or album instead of typing, reducing friction and making the app more convenient, especially for those on the go or users with accessibility needs.

After Usability Test

Revision #3: Shuffle play

To enhance the listening experience, I added a Shuffle Play option on playlists. This gives users more control and variety, allowing them to enjoy their music in a fresh, randomized order without having to manually rearrange tracks.

Solution

Solution

Solution

Chime was designed as an accessible and affordable music streaming platform that addresses the limitations of traditional streaming services. By combining free music streaming, a royalty-free music library, and an affordable premium subscription, the platform offers users greater flexibility without sacrificing the listening experience.

Free & Affordable Music Access

Chime provides free music streaming alongside an affordable premium subscription, making music accessible to budget-conscious listeners while offering premium benefits such as ad-free listening and enhanced playback.

Smart Music Discovery

Users can quickly find songs through voice search, discover new artists, browse curated playlists, and enjoy personalized music exploration with a simple and intuitive interface.

Enhanced Playlist & Playback Experience

Chime gives users greater control over their listening experience with features such as Add to Queue, Add to Playlist, Go to Artist, Go to Album, Copy Song Link, Shuffle Play, and Repeat, making music management seamless and enjoyable.

Results

Results

Results

The final Chime prototype demonstrates how a user-centered design process can transform research insights into a functional and engaging music streaming experience. By validating ideas through usability testing and refining the design based on user feedback, the final solution became more intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs.

Improved User Experience

A clean interface, intuitive navigation, and streamlined user flows make discovering music and managing playlists simple and enjoyable.

Enhanced Functionality

New features such as Voice Search, Shuffle Play, and expanded song and playlist options provide users with greater convenience and control.

Accessible & Modern Design

A consistent visual identity, accessible interface, and responsive design create an inclusive experience that is both visually appealing and easy to use across devices.

Takeaways

Takeaways

Takeaways

Designing Chime was a valuable learning experience that strengthened both my design process and problem-solving skills. Beyond creating a polished interface, I gained a deeper understanding of how music streaming platforms work and how thoughtful features can shape a seamless and enjoyable listening experience.

Designing with Users in Mind

This project reminded me to put users at the center of every design decision. Research and usability testing helped me better understand their needs and create solutions that genuinely improve their experience.

Understanding Music Streaming Experiences

Exploring existing music platforms taught me what users expect from a modern streaming app. From playlist management and music discovery to voice search, playback controls, and premium features, I learned how each feature contributes to creating a smooth and engaging listening experience.

Growing as a Designer

Chime helped me become more confident in balancing aesthetics, accessibility, and functionality. It reinforced the importance of designing products that are not only visually appealing but also intuitive, practical, and enjoyable to use.
