Project Overview

Pawmily is a digital platform designed to simplify and enhance the pet adoption process. The app connects potential pet adopters with shelters, rescue organizations, and foster homes, making it easier to find a furry companion that fits their lifestyle. With its intuitive design and user-friendly features, Pawmily promotes responsible pet adoption while supporting animal welfare communities.

Timeline

From explorations to final designs in approximately 6 weeks.

The Challenge

Adopting a pet is often a long, confusing, and overwhelming process. Many potential adopters struggle with scattered information, outdated listings, and unclear requirements from different shelters and organizations. On the other hand, animal shelters face difficulties in showcasing their pets effectively and managing large volumes of inquiries. This disconnect not only slows down the adoption process but also leaves many animals waiting longer to find their forever homes. Pawmily was created to bridge this gap, offering a platform that makes adoption easier, more transparent, and more accessible for everyone involved.

Objectives

To create a seamless, trustworthy, and compassionate platform that connects potential adopters with shelters and rescue groups. By centralizing adoptable pet profiles, simplifying the application process, and offering educational resources, the app aims to:

  • Make pet adoption more accessible, transparent, and efficient.

  • Empower shelters and rescues with tools to manage and promote their adoptable pets.

  • Encourage responsible pet ownership by providing guidance and resources to adopters.

  • Ultimately, reduce the number of homeless animals by helping more pets find safe, loving, and permanent homes.

Process

This category details the step-by-step approach taken during the project, including research, planning, ideation, design, testing, and refinement phases.

Research & Planning

To design an adoption platform that truly meets the needs of both adopters and shelters, we began by conducting user research to uncover common frustrations, motivations, and expectations in the pet adoption journey. Through interviews and surveys, we developed user personas that represented key groups. These personas helped us frame clear problem statements, highlighting issues like the lack of centralized information, difficulties in comparing pets across platforms, and the emotional stress of unclear adoption processes. A competitive audit of existing adoption apps and websites further revealed gaps in usability, transparency, and user engagement. Guided by these insights, we moved into the ideation phase, where we explored solutions that could simplify the adoption journey, enhance trust, and create a more supportive experience for both adopters and animal welfare organizations.

Mobile App Design

I focused on designing the mobile app, exploring the experience through paper sketches, digital wireframes, and mid-fidelity prototypes. I prioritized quick pet discovery, location-based searching, filters, and clear pet profiles to make it easy for users to find adoptable pets near them.

Usability Testing & Design Refinement

I tested the early prototype to understand how users interacted with the main features and where they experienced difficulties. Based on the findings, I revised the navigation, search and filtering experience, and adoption flow before developing the final UI and high-fidelity type.

Responsive Website Design

After completing the mobile app, I adapted the experience into a responsive website for desktop and mobile screens. I created a sitemap and redesigned the layouts to take advantage of larger screens while maintaining consistency with the app, then developed mid- and high-fidelity prototypes to refine the responsive experience.

Competitive Analysis

To identify gaps and opportunities in the pet adoption landscape, I conducted a competitive audit of existing platforms such as Petfinder and local shelter websites. While these platforms provide extensive pet listings, many lack user-friendly navigation, personalized recommendations, or streamlined application processes. Some rely heavily on outdated data, leading to frustration for adopters, while others offer limited tools for shelters to manage inquiries effectively. These findings revealed a clear opportunity for Pawmily to differentiate itself by combining intuitive design, real-time updates, and integrated tools that serve both adopters and shelters, creating a more supportive and efficient adoption experience.

Mobile App Design

Ideation

In the ideation stage, I used paper sketches to explore different layouts for the app, focusing on how users would search for pets and view available listings. I experimented with home screen structures that highlighted search filters, categories, and nearby pets, making it easy for adopters to quickly find companions that matched their lifestyle. Sketching also helped me test layouts for pet profiles, ensuring key details such as age, health, and location were clear at a glance.

Mid-fidelity Wireframes

When I started building the digital wireframes, my goal was to translate the initial sketches into clear structures that captured the core functions of the app. I focused on designing a clean layout for the home screen with prominent search and filter options, as well as sections that highlighted pets available nearby. For the pet profiles, I prioritized readability by organizing key details like age, health status, and location in a way that adopters could quickly scan. At this stage, I kept the design minimal and low-detail, concentrating on functionality and flow rather than visuals, which made it easier to refine the structure before moving into high-fidelity wireframes.

Mid-fidelity Wireframes

Mid-fidelity Prototype

After building the digital wireframes, I connected the screens into a mid-fidelity prototype to simulate the core user flows. This allowed me to test how adopters would search for pets, apply filters, and browse nearby listings, as well as how shelters could showcase pet details. By keeping the prototype simple and without visual polish, I was able to focus on functionality and navigation. This stage helped me quickly identify pain points in the flow and gather early feedback before moving into more detailed design work.

Design Iteration

After gathering insights from the usability study, I revised the wireframes and low-fidelity prototype to address the issues users encountered. With these refinements, I moved into the high-fidelity design, applying the final color palette, typography, and visual style. The result was a realistic, interactive prototype that not only incorporated usability feedback but also captured the approachable and trustworthy personality I wanted Pawmily to convey.

Iteration #1: Adding Range Limit & Read Section

For the first revision, I added a range limit to help users filter pets by distance, making it easier to find adoptable pets nearby. I also included a dedicated "Read" section with resources and guides to support adopters with helpful information throughout the adoption journey.

Iteration #2: Adding an easy list search browsing option

In the second revision, I introduced a "Reserve" option that allows users to express interest in a pet before finalizing the adoption process. This feature helps streamline communication with shelters and gives adopters peace of mind knowing their chosen pet is being considered for them.

UI Design

When I started working on the UI design, my goal was to make the app feel warm, approachable, and easy to use. I chose a clean layout with soft colors, rounded elements, and playful yet professional typography to reflect the friendly nature of pet adoption. I also paid attention to hierarchy, ensuring that important details like the pet's age, health, and location stood out at a glance. By balancing visual appeal with clarity, I aimed to create an interface that not only looked inviting but also made the adoption process feel less intimidating and more joyful.

Accessibility Considerations

I considered accessibility throughout the design process to make Pawmily easier and more comfortable to use for a wider range of people. I focused on creating a clear interface, readable content, and simple interactions so users can navigate the app and website without unnecessary difficulty.

Clear Color Contrast

I used sufficient contrast between text, icons, and backgrounds to improve readability, especially for users with visual impairments.

Readable Typography

I selected legible font sizes and maintained a clear visual hierarchy so important information, such as pet details and adoption steps, is easy to understand.

Accessible Touch Targets

I designed buttons and interactive elements with appropriately sized touch areas to make them easier to tap on mobile devices.

Simple & Consistent Navigation

I kept navigation patterns and layouts consistent across screens, helping users understand where they are and move through the adoption process with less confusion.

Solution

Solution

Results

Results

Results

Pawmily brought together the key ideas and feedback from the design process into a more focused and approachable adoption experience. The final design helped address common challenges around finding pets, understanding listings, and navigating the adoption journey.

Easier Pet Discovery

The location-based search, filters, and organized listings make it easier for users to find pets that match their preferences and are available near them.

Smoother Adoption Experience

Clearer navigation and a more structured adoption flow help reduce confusion and make it easier for users to move from discovering a pet to expressing interest.

Consistent Across Devices

The responsive website extends the Pawmily experience beyond the app, allowing users to browse and explore adoptable pets comfortably on mobile, tablet, or desktop.

Takeaways

Takeaways

Takeaways

Working on Pawmily became more meaningful to me as I learned more about pet adoption especially how it works in the online space. I started the project thinking mainly about the experience of finding a pet, but the process helped me see how many challenges still exist in making adoption simple and accessible online.

Learning About Pet Adoption

This project taught me a lot about the adoption journey, from searching for available pets to understanding their needs, requirements, and suitability for a new home. It made me realize that adopting a pet involves much more than simply finding a cute animal but also requires the right information and preparation.

There's Still A Gap in Digital Adoption Tools

While researching existing adoption platforms, I noticed that many apps and websites could still do more to make the experience simple, modern, and user-friendly. This made me appreciate the opportunity Pawmily has to provide a more organized way to discover pets, especially through location-based search and clearer adoption information.

Design Can Help Make Adoption Easier

Pawmily showed me that even small design decisions can make a complicated process feel less intimidating. I enjoyed figuring out how features like filters, nearby listings, clear pet profiles, and guided flows could help people feel more confident when looking for a new companion.
